FAQ
Why is a modification slot locked?
A suit or weapon unlocks one slot per grade above G1. Locked slots stay visible so you can plan ahead, and a modification kept in one is held until the grade comes back.
Do material costs include upgrading?
No. Material requirements cover one application of each fitted modification. Grade upgrade costs are paid separately at a settlement.
Where are my loadouts stored?
In this browser. Clearing site data deletes them, so export or share anything you want to keep.
